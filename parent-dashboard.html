Not a setting. Locked for every youth account.
  • No member can message a youth account. There is no inbox, buddies included.
  • No adult can connect with, follow, or add a youth account.
  • Young members can only become buddies when both parents approve.
  • Youth profiles are not searchable or browsable.
  • No profile photos on youth accounts.
  • No location beyond state, and it is never displayed.

You cannot turn these off, and neither can we. That is the point.

On their 18th birthday, this account converts to a full adult account automatically. Messaging and connecting unlock, the youth restrictions come off, and it is no longer linked to yours.
