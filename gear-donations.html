Donate Gear

Give it new life. Teach a kid a trade while you are at it.

Old rods, reels, and tackle sitting in the garage can still put a fish on the line for somebody. If it is used but has life left in it, donate it. Our kids learn to clean, tune up, and refurbish it before it goes back out to a family that needs it.

How it works

1. You tell us what you have got

Working or gently used rods, reels, and tackle, anything with some life left in it. Fill out the form below and we will let you know if it is a good fit.

2. We arrange pickup or mailing

We will message you back to work out whatever is easiest. No address to look up, no guessing.

3. Kids learn, gear gets a second life

Our mentoring kids clean it up and learn real skills doing it. Once it is ready, it goes to a family that needs it.

Why It Matters

Idle hands are the devil's workshop.

Teaching a kid to fix something with their own two hands beats a screen every time. This program gives them a reason to focus, a skill they will keep for life, and a way to give back before they have even had their first paycheck.
