What is Coming

Three kinds of tournaments, all starting soon.

Not one event with everybody thrown in together. Different people are fishing for different reasons, and the format ought to match.

Adult Tournaments

Open competition for grown anglers. Live bait and artificial run as separate classes so nobody has an unfair edge. Entry fees go back into the community the tournament was fished in.

Kids Tournaments

Young anglers fishing their own classes, not competing against adults. Participation counts alongside biggest catch, and every kid who shows up takes something home. Parents fish alongside or watch from the bank.

Veterans Tournaments

Veterans fishing with veterans. A day on the water with people who understand without anything needing to be explained. Open to all branches and all eras.

What They Look Like

More than a weigh-in.

Food, family, a board everybody can get on, and a reason to be outside with your neighbors. Kids fish alongside adults in their own classes. Live bait and artificial run separately, for everybody.

House Rules

How we compete

Separate classes

Live bait and artificial run as separate classes, for kids and adults alike.

No snagging

Snagging a fish on purpose is not fishing. That one is not negotiable.

Everybody places

Kids get participation trophies alongside tracked accomplishments. Showing up counts.
