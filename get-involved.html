Everybody has something to offer.

You do not have to be a good fisherman. You might not have to fish at all.

Ways to pitch in

Teach an adult

Plenty of grown folks never learned. Teaching a 40-year-old to read water is not a consolation prize.

Conservation work

Cleanups, stocking days, habitat projects. Hard work, good company.

Everything else

Cooking, driving, photography, bookkeeping, welding, organizing, fundraising. All of it is needed.

Partners

We would rather connect than compete.

If you run a fishing business, a conservation group, a church, or a school program, we want to work with you and not around you. Every square is a rectangle, but not every rectangle is a square. You stay exactly who you are and still be part of this.
