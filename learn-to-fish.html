Safety First

🎣 Learn this before you go.

You need to know how to stay safe before you put a line in the water. These cover what to wear, how to hold a rod, and what to do when something goes wrong.

⛑️ Kids Fishing Safety From Take Me Fishing. Safety tips for young anglers. 📺 Kids Teaching Kids (Video) From Trout Unlimited. Kids showing kids how to fish safely. 🎬 How to Fish (Easy for Kids!) Video from The Kidsplainer. Start to finish, made for kids.
⚡ Quick Tip: The number one rule is simple. Tell a grown-up where you are going, and take a buddy. Never fish alone.

Follow the Rules

📋 The rules, plain and simple.

Pennsylvania has rules so the fish stay healthy and everybody gets a fair shot. Here is what you need to know.

🎉 Kids under 16: You do not need a fishing license in Pennsylvania. None. You can go fishing today. If you want one anyway, a Voluntary Youth License is $2.97, and the Mentored Youth Permit is free. At 16 you need a real one.
🎟️ Get Your Fishing License Official PA site. Find out if you need a license and how to get one. ❓ Fishing FAQ Questions answered about licenses, seasons, size limits, and more 🌍 Pennsylvania Fishing Guide One-stop guide to PA fishing from Take Me Fishing
🔔 Remember: Check the season dates and size limits before you go. They change by water and by fish. The PA Fish and Boat Commission site has all of it.

Ready to Go?

🚀 Your first trip, start to finish.

Here is what to do before you cast a line.

  1. Watch one safety video. Pick any one from the top of this page.
  2. Learn one fish. Trout is a good place to start in Pennsylvania.
  3. Check if you need a license. Under 16 in Pennsylvania, you do not. Sixteen and up, you do.
  4. Find a buddy or a mentor. Never go alone. Learn from somebody who has done it.
  5. Join a Fishing Friends Network chapter. Local people will show you where the fish are.
  6. Go fishing!
  7. Log your catch. Write down what you caught while you still remember it.
