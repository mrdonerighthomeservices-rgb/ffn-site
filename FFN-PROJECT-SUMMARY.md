# Fishing Friends Network

## The mission

**Fishing, Faith, and Fun for Everyone.**

## Why we are doing this

A kid sits on a bank with a rod in his hand and nothing happens for twenty minutes. Then something pulls. He learns to wait, and he learns that waiting pays. You cannot teach that in a classroom.

Fishing teaches patience, and it teaches you to keep your mouth shut and pay attention. It also puts two people next to each other with nothing to do but talk. Some of the best conversations of a person's life happen in a boat.

That is what we are building around. It was never about the fish.

## Who this is for

Kids who have nobody to take them. Parents who want to take their kids and do not know how to start. Grown men who have fished for thirty years and have nobody left to fish with. Veterans. Churches. Anybody who wants in.

Faith guides this work. It does not decide who is welcome. If you fish, you belong here.

## What it is

A place to join, learn, and find people to fish with near you.

Joining is free. You get a private catch log, the learning material, the jokes, and the community board. Later there will be a paid tier for people who want more, and that money keeps the lights on.

Chapters run local. One town at a time, led by somebody who lives there.

## What we will not do

We will not take a child under thirteen. The law is clear and so are we.

We will not build something that keeps a kid on a screen. If a kid spends more time on this site than on the water, we built the wrong thing. Everything here should end with somebody going outside.

We will not promise a number we cannot hit. Nothing gets counted before it happens.

## Safety first, ethics second, fishing third

Every member takes a safety test. Ten questions, eight to pass, and you can take it as many times as you need. A kid who misses one learns why before he moves on.

Parents see what their kid is doing. Adults get vetted before they lead anything. We do not cut corners here and we are not going to start.

## What is built and working

Real member accounts with real passwords. Age checked at signup, and under thirteen is refused outright.

A private catch log for every member. Nobody sees it but you. We do not store where you were.

A joke bank that members add to and we approve before it shows. Golden Nuggets, the learning pages, and the safety test. A public board for the fish worth showing off.

The site is blue, plain, and fast. No ads and nothing blinking at you.

## What comes next

Get the free membership working with real people on it. Open the first chapters. Listen to what they tell us.

After that, the paid tier and a way to take payment. Tools so a chapter leader can run his own group without calling us. More learning. An app that does what the site does.

The money side stays honest. Memberships, partners, merchandise, and donations. We are looking at other ways to bring money in and none of them are decided yet.

## How we will know it worked

Not by clicks. By how many kids learned to fish, how many grown men took one of them out, and how many families spent a Saturday on the water instead of in the house.

Somebody taught you. Your turn now.
