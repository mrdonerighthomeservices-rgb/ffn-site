Community Fishing Days

Send your kid to fish with us. You will know exactly how it works.

Group sessions, supervised by multiple adults, out in the open, not one-on-one. This is the same kind of permission slip your kid's school sends home, just online. Free for every family, every time.

How this is different from mentoring

Group Fishing Days

What this page is for. Kids fish together in a group, with multiple adults present and visible the whole time. This is a supervised community event, not a one-on-one relationship.

One-on-One Mentoring

A separate program with its own process. Mentors go through a full background check before ever being paired with a specific child. That is handled through Get Involved, not this form.

Why We Ask

Busy parents deserve a place they can trust.

You should not have to be standing on the bank to know your kid is safe, having fun, and learning something real. This form is how we make sure every adult on-site has what they need to look out for your child: who to call, what to know, and who is allowed to pick them up.

Permission Slip

Sign your kid up for the next Community Fishing Day

Takes about two minutes. This covers one event at a time. You will fill this out for each Community Fishing Day your kid attends, so our records always match who is actually coming.

This permission slip is free for every family, every event. Premium members get a saved family profile that pre-fills most of this automatically. See Premium.
