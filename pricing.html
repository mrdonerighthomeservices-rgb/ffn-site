Ask About Mentoring
What counts as a family, for this plan

Up to two adults and every child living in the household. Not defined as a mother and a father. That covers a mother and father completely, and it also covers a single parent working two jobs, a grandmother raising her grandkids, an aunt, a foster family, or anyone else standing in as the grown-up in a kid's life. Nobody has to read this page and wonder if they qualify.

Premium, In Full

Everything Premium Unlocks

Sixteen real reasons to upgrade, on top of everything already free. Not built yet. This is the roadmap for the next real build, once accounts and login exist.

AI fishing assistant

Ask it anything. What is biting, what to tie on, how to read the water.

Advanced maps & water data

Depth, structure, and conditions beyond the free basics.

Weather + bite predictor

Real NOAA/USGS data, run through the AI assistant.

Personal best tracker

Every record you have set, all in one place.

Private trip log & journal

Your own notes, kept private, trip by trip.

Personal catch log

What worked, where, and under what conditions, searchable later.

Trip planner

Pick a date, get weather and condition suggestions.

Private buddy groups

Family, church, or crew circles, not just one public feed.

Discussion board

Gear talk, tips, and general fishing chat. Public posts only, same safety rules as everywhere else. No private messaging, ever.

Status updates

Facebook-style posts like "heading out later" or "got skunked." A place name you type in yourself, never your GPS location.

Digital badges

Tied to your skill tier: Goober, Club Member, Semi-Pro, Pro.

Custom nickname flair

A cosmetic badge or title tied to your skill tier.

"Ask a Mentor" Q&A

Post a question, a real mentor answers it publicly. Premium revenue tied directly to the mentoring mission.

Print-your-year recap

A shareable summary of your whole season.

Joke of the Day

The best of the fishing & outdoor jokes bank, pushed straight to you first.

Saved family profile

Pre-fills your Community Fishing Day permission slips automatically, so no retyping emergency contacts or medical info every time. The permission slip itself is always free for every family; this just saves you time if you come back often.

Same safety rules apply everywhere on FFN, free or Premium: no home location or GPS is ever shown publicly, and a "report a post" button is on every feed from day one.
