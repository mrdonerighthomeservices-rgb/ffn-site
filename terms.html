The plain-English rules for being here.

The short version

Be honest, be respectful, and do not use FFN to hurt or take advantage of anyone, especially kids. We will do our best to run a good program, but we cannot promise outcomes, and we are not liable for what happens away from the site, like on a fishing trip or at an event.

If you are a parent signing up a child, you are the one agreeing to these terms on their behalf.

Who can use FFN

Adults sign up for themselves. Anyone under 13 is signed up by a parent or guardian, who manages the account. Anyone 13 to 17 needs a parent or guardian's permission before joining.

By creating an account, you are confirming the information you gave us is accurate, and that you have the right to agree to these terms, either for yourself or for a child in your care.

What we expect from members

  • Treat other members, especially young members, with respect.
  • No harassment, bullying, hate speech, or inappropriate contact of any kind.
  • No posting of another person's private information, including a child's location or last name.
  • No using FFN to promote, sell, or advertise anything unrelated to fishing or the FFN community, without our permission.
  • Follow local fishing laws and regulations. FFN does not grant you any license or permit, and you are responsible for your own compliance.

We can remove content, suspend an account, or end a membership if these rules are not followed. For anything involving a young member's safety, we will act first and explain after, if needed.

Mentoring and volunteers

Mentors go through a background check process before being connected with any family. Even so, FFN does not guarantee the conduct of any volunteer or mentor, and does not make placement decisions between adults and children. Background check results are shared with parents so they can make their own informed decision.

Participation in any FFN activity, tournament, or outing is voluntary, and it is each family's responsibility to decide what is appropriate for their child.

You keep ownership of anything you post, like a photo, a catch, or a comment. By posting it, you are giving FFN permission to show it on the site and use it to promote FFN, like on the Lunker Board or in materials about the program. You can ask us to remove your content at any time.

No photo of a minor is used publicly without a parent or guardian's written permission first, separate from these terms.
