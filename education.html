Learning Library

Free, and from people who know.

We would rather point you to the folks already doing it well than pretend we wrote the book.

Start with your state

Fishing laws are set by each state, not by us. Size limits, creel limits, seasons, and which species you can keep all change from state to state and they change most years. Pick your state and we will send you straight to the official source.

Open the official site

We link to your state agency instead of printing limits here on purpose. Regulations change, sometimes mid-season, and a wrong number on our site could cost you a fine. The official book is always right. We are not.

LicensesNearly every state requires one for adults. Age at which a license becomes required varies. Buy directly from your state agency, never a third party.
The Lacey ActFederal law on transporting fish and wildlife across state lines. Matters if you fish more than one state.Adults
US Coast Guard boating safetyLife jackets, required equipment, and boating law. The official source.
National Weather Service safetyLightning, flooding, cold water, and heat. Free, official, updated constantly.
Cold water and wadingCold water kills faster than most people expect, even in summer. Never wade alone and never wade what you cannot see the bottom of.Read first
Hooks and handlingBarbless hooks come out easier, out of you and out of the fish. Pliers in the boat, always.Good for kids
USGS water dataReal-time river levels, flow, and temperature for thousands of gauges. Free.
EPA fish consumption advisoriesWhich fish are safe to eat from which waters, and how often. Matters most for kids and pregnant women.
Aquatic invasive speciesClean, drain, dry. How not to move a problem from one lake to the next.
Catch and release done rightWet your hands, support the belly, keep it in the water, and put it back facing into the current until it swims off on its own.
Ready.govFederal emergency preparedness. Weather, gear, and what to do when things go sideways.
National Park Service outdoor safetyWildlife encounters, getting lost, water, and heat.
Tell somebody where you are goingThe single most useful survival habit there is, and it costs nothing. Where you are putting in, where you are taking out, and when to worry.
Your first knotsImproved clinch, palomar, loop knot. Fifteen minutes and you can fish.Good for kids

Handling and releaseKeeping a fish alive that you intend to put back.Good for kids
